## MadokaChanger 1.2.1

MadokaChanger now patches only your current locked-in champion, plus your Duo partner's champion when they have a supported matching selection.

- Detects completed lock-ins through the League client. Hovering another champion does not apply a patch; trades and finalized ARAM swaps update the detected champion.
- Keeps other saved skins and enabled custom packages in your collection without including them in the current build.
- Press **Start** once. If you have not locked in or have no applicable saved skin, the session waits without launching a patcher host.
- Press **Apply Selection** after choosing a new skin, chroma or form. An active patcher stops and restarts automatically with the new selection. This also works without a Duo party.
- Changes during a running game wait until the game closes. **Stop** cancels waiting and pending automatic work.

Existing users can use **Update**, or run **MadokaChanger-Setup-1.2.1.exe**. Settings, saved selections and downloaded skins are preserved.

Verification covers LCU hover/lock/trade/dodge/ARAM fixtures, solo and Duo source filtering, actual fake-host stop/start sequences, waiting and resumption, game-safe deferral, explicit Stop intent, off-screen WPF layouts and the live relay with simulated League state. Live in-game appearance remains unverified. The installer is unsigned.
